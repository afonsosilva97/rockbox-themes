Iris R3 Preview

The first build of iris-r3, rendered by the Rockbox UI simulator for the R3 Pro II. The simulator runs the same skin engine as the player, so these screens show the theme as it draws on the device.

Touch controls

  • Tap the play button to play or pause. Hold it to stop.
  • Tap previous or next to skip. Hold to rewind or fast-forward.
  • Tap shuffle or repeat to toggle. Repeat cycles off, all, one.
  • Tap or drag along the seek bar to jump.
  • Hold the album art for the track's context menu.
  • On Now Playing, tap the top bars to open the menu, or hold them to lock the screen.
  • In menus, tap the title bar to go back.

What changed from Iris

  • The vertical sidebar became a 36 px status strip across the top.
  • Album art is 338 px instead of 84 px.
  • Added touch controls, shuffle and repeat, which Iris didn't need on a click wheel.
  • The lock screen works with a touch lock, because this player has no hold switch.
  • Inter converted again at sizes for a 218 ppi screen, anti-aliased.

Still to check on the player

  • Text sizes feel right at arm's length.
  • Touch targets respond reliably to a finger.
  • Volume bar range and battery fill match the real levels.
  • Speed of scrolling and redraws on the hardware.
