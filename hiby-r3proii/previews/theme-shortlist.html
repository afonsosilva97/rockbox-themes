UImiz Revamped

Port

A dark purple redesign of the Ulmiz family: large album art, lavender pill rows for album, artist and title, and a glowing play button.

Made for
HIDIZS AP80 Pro Max, 360×640 portrait
Authors
mr-f0xx, based on Ulmiz by Simon Rothen and JGUlmiz by jgoedde
Licence
CC BY-SA 3.0. A port is allowed with credit, under the same licence.
What it takes
  • The screen is wider (×1.33) and only slightly taller (×1.125), so every position is recalculated instead of simply scaled.
  • Its bitmaps are drawn for 360 px. JGUlmiz has 480 px versions of the shared artwork, which could be recoloured purple instead of upscaling.
Source
github.com/mr-f0xx/UImiz-Revamped-AP80-Pro-Max

CrazyBit Tube Edition

Port

Retro amber-on-black, like a vacuum-tube amplifier display. Pixel type, boxed panels and butterfly VU meters that move with the music.

Made for
HIDIZS AP80 Pro Max, 360×640 portrait
Authors
Imre Ferenczi (mr-f0xx), based on CrazyBit by Roman Mamedov and CrazyBitMono by ottoptj
Licence
CC BY-SA 3.0. A port is allowed with credit, under the same licence.
What it takes
  • Pixel art and pixel fonts don't scale cleanly by 1.33, so the panels and type need redrawing at the new size to stay crisp.
  • Most of the look is drawn with skin rectangles rather than bitmaps, which helps.
  • The VU meters use Rockbox's built-in peak meter, so they react to the music on the official build.
  • Ships 30 MB of fonts. Only the sizes actually used need to come across.
Source
github.com/mr-f0xx/CrazyBitTube-AP80-Pro-Max

Iris

Redesign

Quiet and minimal in the Rosé Pine palette, with four colour variants. A small accent dot doubles as the play-state indicator on every screen.

Made for
320×240 landscape (EROS Q and K, iPod Video and others)
Author
gloceansh. Fonts from Inter (SIL OFL), palette by Rosé Pine.
Licence
MIT. The most permissive of the four.
What it takes
  • Landscape to portrait, so the layout is rebuilt. Its sidebar would become a top bar, as in the concept.
  • Nearly everything is drawn with skin primitives instead of bitmaps, so the redesign is mostly skin code, not new artwork.
  • Inter needs converting to larger bitmap font sizes for this screen.
Source
github.com/gloceandotdev/iris-rockbox-theme

Adwaitapod

Inspiration only

A polished GNOME-style interface: clean light and dark modes, rounded cards, blue accents and a quick-settings panel.

Made for
iPod Classic 5th to 7th generation, 320×240 landscape
Author
D0-0K
Licence
GPL-3.0. Redistribution is allowed, but anything derived from it must also be GPL-3.0.
What it takes
  • Built around the click wheel and a landscape screen, so nothing carries over directly. Every screen is designed again for portrait touch.
  • Very feature-rich (lock screen, multiple layouts, languages), so even a partial take is a large project.
Source
github.com/D0-0K/adwaitapod
