Iris

Redesign

Quiet and minimal in the Rosé Pine palette, with four colour variants. A small accent dot doubles as the play-state indicator on every screen.

Made for
320×240 landscape (EROS Q and K, iPod Video and others)
Author
gloceansh. Fonts from Inter (SIL OFL), palette by Rosé Pine.
Licence
MIT. The more permissive of the two.
What it takes
  • Landscape to portrait, so the layout is rebuilt. Its sidebar would become a top bar, as in the concept.
  • Nearly everything is drawn with skin primitives instead of bitmaps, so the redesign is mostly skin code, not new artwork.
  • Inter needs converting to larger bitmap font sizes for this screen.
Source
github.com/gloceandotdev/iris-rockbox-theme

Adwaitapod

Inspiration only

A polished GNOME-style interface: clean light and dark modes, rounded cards, blue accents and a quick-settings panel.

Made for
iPod Classic 5th to 7th generation, 320×240 landscape
Author
D0-0K
Licence
GPL-3.0. Redistribution is allowed, but anything derived from it must also be GPL-3.0.
What it takes
  • Built around the click wheel and a landscape screen, so nothing carries over directly. Every screen is designed again for portrait touch.
  • Very feature-rich (lock screen, multiple layouts, languages), so even a partial take is a large project.
Source
github.com/D0-0K/adwaitapod
